OPTIONAL CONTRIBUTION · VERSION 3

Learning contribution terms

Sharing is optional. DREAM AI does not upload an exchange for learning merely because you use chat.

What happens when you press the contribution button

Only the single question and the corresponding DREAM AI answer shown beside that button are submitted. Your other chat messages, saved browser memory, account identity and conversation history are not included.

Permission you grant

By pressing the contribution button, you confirm that you wrote the submitted material or have permission from its rights-holder to contribute it for this purpose. You grant the DREAM AI project a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to store, reproduce, format, modify and use this exact exchange to evaluate, improve, build and train DREAM AI, including supervised fine-tuning and model development. You expressly permit DREAM AI and models trained using this exchange to be used in free or paid offerings and in commercial services that DREAM AI may operate. This permission applies only to the one exchange shown beside the button; it does not cover your other chats or saved memory. You keep ownership of material you own. Do not submit material you are not authorized to license for this use.

Possible use and review

The submitted exchange is stored as a private, pending candidate. It may be used to improve or train DREAM AI only after separate human review. Reviewers must independently check the content's rights and permission, privacy, safety and quality. A contribution click does not automatically approve an example or start training. If a trained model later powers a paid service, the specific exchange may have influenced that model's parameters.

Contributions are not intended for public display or training another provider's model. They are kept in the private DREAM AI learning store for this project. No payment is offered for contributions.

Temporary session grouping

A temporary random identifier is used to group exchanges shared during the same browser-tab session. This helps keep related examples together when creating training and validation datasets. The raw identifier is not saved in the database; the ingestion service stores a one-way SHA-256 fingerprint instead. This fingerprint is pseudonymous, not anonymous, and is not an account or person identifier.

What you should not submit

  • Passwords, API keys, private access codes or other secrets.
  • Personal, confidential, sensitive or identifying information about yourself or another person.
  • Third-party copyrighted or licensed material unless you have permission for this specific use.
  • Content you do not have the right to contribute.

Human review and model training

Candidate examples remain excluded from training until a human reviewer separately confirms permission, privacy, quality and explicit training approval. Approved examples are split into separate training and validation groups before supervised fine-tuning. A model checkpoint is evaluated separately before it can be considered for release.

Not every submitted exchange will be approved or used. The project may reject examples that are unsafe, inaccurate, low quality, duplicated, private, unlicensed or unsuitable for training.

Important limitation

Once information has been incorporated into a trained model, removing its influence may not be straightforward and can require further model training. Do not contribute anything that you need to remain confidential or that you would not be comfortable contributing to the development of DREAM AI.

Your choice

You can continue using DREAM AI without contributing any examples. The contribution button is a separate, voluntary action for one exchange at a time.
